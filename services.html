01 — Brand & expression

Get clear on who you are, then show up that way.

Positioning, content and social presence built as one system rather than three separate deliverables.

S01 / Strategy

Brand strategy & positioning

We define who you are for, what you stand for and why you should be chosen. The output is a practical decision-making framework your team can use — not a decorative document.

  • Audience and competitor landscape
  • Positioning and category narrative
  • Messaging architecture and tone of voice
  • Value proposition and proof points
  • Brand guidelines and decision rules
  • Roadmap for expression across channels
S02 / Creative

Creative & content

Content that carries the positioning into the real world. We plan, write and design across the assets your brand needs to sell, explain and stay memorable.

  • Content strategy and editorial calendar
  • Website and landing page copy
  • Product and collection storytelling
  • Campaign concepts and ad creative
  • Email and lifecycle content
  • Visual asset direction and design coordination
S03 / Social

Social media

A consistent, purposeful presence on the channels that matter to your audience — with a plan for what each platform is actually meant to achieve.

  • Channel and format strategy
  • Content pillars and posting cadence
  • Caption and creative production
  • Community and comment management
  • Creator and collaboration coordination
  • Performance review and iteration
02 — Growth & acquisition

Demand you can measure, and keep.

Organic and paid acquisition planned together, so each channel feeds the next instead of competing for the same credit.

S04 / Marketing

Digital marketing

End-to-end marketing management across the channels your customers actually use. We plan the mix, run the work and keep the message consistent from first touch to purchase.

  • Channel strategy and budget allocation
  • Campaign planning and calendars
  • Email and lifecycle campaigns
  • Landing page and offer planning
  • Partnership and influencer coordination
  • Campaign reporting and learnings
S05 / Organic

SEO & organic growth

Build a search presence that compounds. We work on the technical foundation, the content and the authority signals together — because one without the other stalls.

  • Technical SEO audits and fixes
  • Keyword and intent mapping
  • Site architecture and internal linking
  • Content briefs and optimisation
  • Structured data and indexation
  • Ranking, traffic and conversion tracking
S06 / Paid

Paid advertising

Paid media managed against real business outcomes, not just clicks. We align creative, targeting, landing pages and measurement before scaling spend.

  • Account structure and audit
  • Audience and campaign strategy
  • Ad creative and copy coordination
  • Bid, budget and pacing management
  • Conversion tracking and attribution
  • Testing roadmap and scale decisions
03 — Commerce & retention

From first click to fifth order.

Your store and customer system are where growth becomes revenue. We manage the whole path, including what happens after checkout.

S07 / Commerce

E-commerce management

Day-to-day ownership of your store operation: catalogue, merchandising, offers, apps and the small fixes that otherwise pile up. For platform-specific Shopify work, our sister division Ziddi Techies .IN handles the deep technical side.

  • Store and catalogue operations
  • Product data, imagery and merchandising
  • Promotions and campaign setup
  • App and integration coordination
  • Store QA and issue resolution
  • Operational checklists and handovers
S08 / Conversion

Conversion optimization

Find where interest leaks out of your funnel and fix it in a structured way. Every change starts with a hypothesis and ends with a measurement.

  • Funnel and usability audits
  • Landing page and product page improvements
  • Cart and checkout friction analysis
  • Trust signals and objection handling
  • A/B testing plans
  • Post-change measurement and iteration
S09 / Retention

CRM & retention

Turn a customer list into an asset. We design the lifecycle journeys, segments and messages that bring people back without relying on constant discounting.

  • Customer segmentation and tagging
  • Welcome and post-purchase journeys
  • Replenishment and win-back flows
  • Loyalty and referral programme support
  • Email and messaging calendar
  • Retention metrics and cohort reporting
04 — Operations & intelligence

The part most teams forget to fund.

Automation, reporting and sensible use of AI make the rest of the work faster, cleaner and easier to see.

S10 / Technology

Technology & automation

Connect the tools your brand already uses and remove the manual steps between them. We focus on practical workflows your team will actually adopt.

  • Tool stack and workflow audit
  • App and platform integration
  • Automated data sync and alerts
  • Inventory, order and catalogue workflows
  • Internal dashboards and documentation
  • Troubleshooting and maintenance
S11 / AI

AI-assisted operations

Use AI where it produces real leverage — research, drafts, analysis and repetitive operations — with human review before anything reaches a customer.

  • Workflow and use-case identification
  • AI-assisted research and content drafts
  • Catalogue and product data enrichment
  • Reporting summaries and insights
  • Prompt and process documentation
  • Quality, brand-safety and privacy checks

Reporting & growth strategy

One view of what is working, what is not and what we do next. Reporting exists to support decisions, not to fill a slide deck.

  • Dashboard setup and maintenance
