Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. This page explains what happens to information you send us through this website.

What we collect

When you contact us, we receive the details you choose to provide: your name, email address, brand or company name, website URL, the area you are interested in and your message. If you contact us by WhatsApp or email, we receive the content of that message and your sender details.

Why we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry, understand your requirements, manage any engagement that follows and keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing lists.

How the website handles forms

This site is hosted on Netlify. Enquiries submitted through the contact form are processed by Netlify Forms and delivered to the Ziddi Techies .COM team. Standard server logs may record technical information such as IP address and browser type as part of normal website operation and security.

Third-party services

  • Netlify — hosting and form delivery.
  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded when pages are displayed; Google may receive standard request data such as IP address.
  • WhatsApp — if you choose to contact us there, the conversation is handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy terms.

Our sister site zidditechies.in is a separate business division with its own practices.

Retention and your choices

We keep enquiry records only as long as they are useful for responding to you and managing any resulting work, unless we are required to keep them longer. You can ask us to access, correct or delete the details you sent by emailing support@zidditechies.com. We will handle reasonable requests promptly.

Children

This website is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site or our processes change. The date at the top will always show the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@zidditechies.com or write to Ziddi Techies .COM.
