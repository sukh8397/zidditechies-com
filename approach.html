Our approach

You should never have to manage the handoffs.

Brand work fails in the gaps between strategy, creative, media, store and data. Our operating model is designed to close those gaps — with one roadmap, one point of contact and visible progress every week.

The cycle

Four steps, on repeat.

A simple rhythm that keeps strategy and execution connected without adding meetings to your calendar.

01

Discover

We review your brand, channels, store, analytics and current bottlenecks. You share context once, not five times.

02

Plan

We agree a prioritised roadmap with scope, owners and measures. Nothing starts before you know what it is for.

03

Execute

Our integrated team delivers in focused sprints across strategy, creative, media, commerce and technology.

04

Improve

We report what changed, review the numbers and use the learning to shape the next sprint.

Communication

One place to ask, decide and review

You get a single point of contact for requests and decisions. Behind that person sits the right specialist for the task — without you needing to know who to ping.

Decision-making

Evidence first, opinion second

We agree what success looks like before work begins. Where the data is thin, we say so and propose a small test rather than a big assumption.

  • ✦Hypotheses before changesEvery optimisation starts with a reason and a measure.
  • ◫Scope agreed upfrontYou approve deliverables and priorities before execution.
  • ↗Human review on AI outputAI assists the work; people stand behind it.
Engagement shapes

Support shaped around your actual need.

The right model depends on where you are. We agree the shape together before any work starts.

Ongoing

Embedded team

Continuous ownership of an agreed set of functions, run against a rolling roadmap with regular reviews.

Best when several parts of the brand need steady, connected work.

Focused

Project sprint

A defined outcome with a clear start and end — a repositioning, a store launch, a campaign or a channel build.

Best when you know exactly what needs to get done.

Advisory

Strategy & guidance

Senior thinking and review without day-to-day execution: audits, priorities and decisions your team implements.

Best when you have internal capacity but need direction.

Frequently asked

Good questions, straight answers.

Do I have to hand over every function at once?
No. Most engagements start with one or two functions where the pain is highest. The roadmap shows how the rest connects when you are ready.
Will you work with my existing team and tools?
Yes. We work inside your existing stack where it makes sense and recommend changes only when a tool or workflow is genuinely holding work back.
What do you need from me to get started?
Access to the relevant accounts, brand context, your goals and one decision-maker who can approve priorities. We provide a clear list during discovery.
How is this different from the Shopify division?
Ziddi Techies .IN focuses on Shopify growth and store management. Ziddi Techies .COM manages the wider brand operation — strategy, marketing, creative, commerce, retention and reporting. They are independent divisions with separate teams and scopes.
How do we agree on success?
We define measures during planning. They might be commercial, operational or channel-specific, but they are agreed and visible before work begins.
