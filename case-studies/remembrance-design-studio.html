The Project

Designing a digital canvas worthy of literary and visual art.

Remembrance Design Studio merges literature, authorship, and tactile design. The digital presence needed to feel like reading an exquisite publication — deliberate pacing, refined serif typography, and tactile whitespace.

Ziddi Techies executed a custom website design and interactive digital showcase, balancing narrative text with architectural layout simplicity.
