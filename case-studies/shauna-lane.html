The Challenge

Showcasing dynamic comedic timing and multi-hyphenate talent.

Shauna Lane is an accomplished actor, stand-up comedian performing across iconic stages like Caroline's on Broadway and New York Comedy Club, and a gifted tutor and storyteller. Her online home needed to capture her warm, hilarious personality while serving as a professional hub for casting directors, comedy festival bookers, and private coaching clients.

Ziddi Techies delivered a sleek, media-rich website: high-impact performance photography, integrated stand-up video reels, and responsive booking inquiries.
