The Challenge

Bridging artisan heritage with modern global luxury.

Daughters of the Ganges works directly with traditional women artisans across India to create handcrafted, ethical jewelry. They needed a digital flagship that could compete with high-end luxury fashion brands globally without losing their grassroots ethical soul and fair-trade integrity.

Ziddi Techies architected their complete digital store: high-contrast typography, rich artisan storytelling sections, currency localization, and frictionless mobile checkout.
