Engineered for the Elements: The Dry Cape

The Challenge

Rugged gear demands an intuitive, high-speed mobile checkout.

Bikers and adventure travelers shopping for heavy-duty rainwear need immediate proof of waterproofing, durability specs, and sizing clarity. The Dry Cape needed an e-commerce platform built to convert on smartphones with high visual contrast.

Ziddi Techies built their complete Shopify architecture: organizing technical product specifications, waterproofing ratings, multi-size SKU listings, and frictionless cart flows.
