The Challenge

A 60-year heritage with no digital footprint.

Raja Products had loyal generational consumers across Delhi NCR and retail counters for over six decades, but lacked a unified modern digital identity. The packaging was dated, no e-commerce store existed, products weren't indexed on Google, and customer acquisition was solely offline word-of-mouth.

Rather than hiring 5 different agencies (a packaging designer, a photoshoot studio, a Shopify agency, an SEO freelancer, and a performance marketing team), the founders partnered with Ziddi Techies .COM as their single operating arm.

Pillar 03 · Store Engineering & Google Console

Complete Shopify build & technical infrastructure.

We built the full online store on Shopify at rajaproducts.in, organizing 25 distinct collections and over 215 product SKUs with lightning-fast navigation.

  • ✓ Google Search Console indexing: Verified domain ownership, submitted dynamic sitemaps, structured schema data for Product & Organization rich snippets.
  • ✓ Tiered Free-Gift Gamification: Customized progressive cart thresholds (Free Imli at ₹499, Free Shipping at ₹999, Free Khakhra at ₹1,499, Free Dry Amla at ₹2,999) pushing AOV higher.
  • ✓ Direct WhatsApp Commerce: One-click WhatsApp checkout and quick inquiry integration for custom bulk gifting.
