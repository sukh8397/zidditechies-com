The Partnership

Handling rapid collection drops without technical downtime.

O'Frida launches limited-edition artisan drops with custom hand-painted prints. In high-frequency fashion e-commerce, slow uploads, broken inventory variants, and sluggish checkout directly kill sales during drop spikes.

Ziddi Techies serves as their ongoing IT partner: handling complete site uploads, setting up complex product variants, managing seasonal banners, optimizing page speed, and resolving technical requirements instantly.
