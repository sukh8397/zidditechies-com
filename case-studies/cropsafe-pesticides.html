The Challenge

Industrial rigor meets clean corporate credibility.

As an agrochemical formulation and plant protection manufacturer, Cropsafe Pesticides required regulatory-compliant bottle and pouch packaging with clear chemical dosages, alongside a corporate portal providing safety sheets, dealer directories, and product formulations.

Ziddi Techies executed their physical label architecture across their crop-protection catalogue, conducted professional bottle cutouts, and developed their responsive corporate website.
