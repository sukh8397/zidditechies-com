The Challenge

Cutting through noisy social feeds with distinct graphic grit.

Gen-Z streetwear brands cannot survive on standard minimalist templates. They require loud, unapologetic graphic design language, high-energy typography, and visual assets built to capture immediate attention in endless social media scrolling.

Ziddi Techies developed Over The Top's digital visual language: gritty social media creative templates, apparel graphics, and website design concepts.
