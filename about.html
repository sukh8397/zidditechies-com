About us

We built the team we wanted to hire.

Ziddi Techies .COM is the brand-management division of Ziddi Techies. It exists for founders and growth teams who are tired of coordinating specialists and want one team responsible for the whole digital picture.

Our point of view

Growth is a system, not a stack of freelancers.

A brand’s positioning, store, content, ads, search presence and customer data all affect each other. When they are managed separately, every handoff costs time and every report answers only part of the question.

We bring those functions together under one roadmap. You still get specialists — they just work as one team instead of as disconnected vendors.

What that means in practice
  • 01One brief, not fiveYou explain the goal once. We translate it for every function involved.
  • 02One prioritised roadmapWork is sequenced so brand, growth and commerce reinforce each other.
  • 03One point of contactA single person owns updates, decisions and escalations.
  • 04One view of progressReporting connects activity to the outcomes we agreed to measure.
Our principles

How we decide, even when nobody is watching.

Clarity

Say the plain thing

No theatre, no jargon for its own sake. If something is unclear, we make it clear before we build on it.

Ownership

Finish the loop

We do not stop at a recommendation. We follow through to execution, measurement and the next decision.

Craft

Sweat the small stuff

Details in a product page, an email or a campaign setup are where brands quietly win or lose.

Evidence

Measure honestly

We agree measures upfront and report what actually happened, including when something did not work.

Leverage

Use tools sensibly

Automation and AI earn their place by removing friction — not by adding novelty.

Focus

Do fewer things better

A short list of priorities with real ownership beats a long list of half-finished initiatives.

Two divisions

Specialist focus, shared standard.

The two businesses are independent and deliberately scoped, so neither team is stretched across incompatible work.
